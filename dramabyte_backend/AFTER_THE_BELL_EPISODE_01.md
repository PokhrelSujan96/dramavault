# After the Bell

## Episode 1: The Empty Seat

Nia, an eighteen-year-old scholarship student, arrives at her new school's cafeteria with a lunch tray. The only empty chair is beside Ethan, a popular final-year student who treats the table as his territory.

Ethan: “That seat isn't for you.”

Nia pauses, hurt but composed.

Nia: “It's the only empty seat.”

An empty blue chair sits beside Ethan's red backpack.

Ethan: “Then eat somewhere else.”

Nia meets his gaze.

Nia: “You don't get to decide that.”

For the first time, Ethan's smirk disappears. Nia is not going to let him decide whether she belongs.
