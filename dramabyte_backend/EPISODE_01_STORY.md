# The Last Train Home

## Episode 1 — The Boy Who Knew Her Name

At eleven forty-seven, Maya boarded the last train home. The carriage was empty, except for a little boy holding a red umbrella.

His coat was soaked. The seat beneath him was dry.

“You’re late, Maya,” he said.

She stopped. She had never seen him before.

“Where are your parents?”

He pointed through the window. Outside, the station lights vanished. Only darkness remained.

Maya reached for her phone. No signal. Then the train began to slow.

A forgotten platform emerged from the rain. She knew this place. It had closed ten years ago, after the accident.

The boy placed a small silver pendant beside her. It was identical to the one around her neck.

“You promised you’d come back,” he whispered.

The doors opened.

On the platform stood another Maya. Same coat. Same pendant. But she was smiling.
